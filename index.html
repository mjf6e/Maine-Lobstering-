Day-by-Day

Sun, Sep 27 — Drive to Waterbury▶

A relaxed driving day. Waterbury is purely an overnight stop — don't try to sightsee.

7:00 AM
Depart Waynesboro
Along the way: breakfast/coffee stop, lunch, two or three short stretch breaks
Late aft.
Arrive Waterbury, check into Hampton Inn
  • Optional: Holy Land USA (~1 hr) only if you still have energy — otherwise rest up for Maine
Evening
Dinner: Sultan's Turkish Restaurant — kebabs, mezze, house bread; a deliberate break before three days of seafood (Alt: La Tavola, Italian)
🌙 Overnight: Hampton Inn Waterbury
Mon, Sep 28 — Portland: The Lobster Boat Day▶
7:00 AM
Breakfast
7:30 AM
Leave Waterbury, drive to Portland
~Noon
Arrive Portland — park near Old Port/Long Wharf, keep lunch light
2:00 PM
🦞 Lucky Catch Lobster Cruise — the highlight of the trip
  • ~80–90 min hauling traps, cruising Casco Bay, passing Portland Head Light & Fort Gorges
  • Buy your catch and have it cooked next door at Portland Lobster Company
3:45 PM
Old Port — Commercial Street, piers, cobblestone streets. Free; about atmosphere, not shopping
5:15 PM
Eastern Promenade — walk along Casco Bay
6:30 PM
Dinner: Scales for a special/celebration dinner on the waterfront, or Luke's Lobster Portland Pier for casual
🌙 Overnight: Hampton Inn & Suites Portland West
Tue, Sep 29 — Portland Head Light → Freeport▶

Your iconic Maine scenery day.

7:30 AM
Breakfast, check out of Portland hotel
8:30 AM
Portland Head Light + Fort Williams Park — #1 land-based must-see
  • Lighthouse viewpoints, rocky shoreline, Cliff Walk, old fortifications, Battery Blair, Battery Keyes
  • Park is free; check parking fees for the date
  • This is the priority — don't rush it to fit anything else in
10:30 AM
Cape Elizabeth Coastal Drive — Two Lights State Park (30–45 min) if time allows
1:00 PM
Drive to Freeport
2:00 PM
Check into Brewster House B&B (180 Main St)
Lunch
🦞 Linda Bean's Maine Kitchen & Topside Tavern — classic lobster roll, right across from L.L.Bean flagship
3:00 PM
Walk Freeport — L.L.Bean flagship (95 Main St, open 24/7). Don't devote the whole afternoon to shopping
5:30 PM
Dinner: Tuscan Brick Oven Bistro — pasta & wood-fired pizza, a deliberate change from seafood
🌙 Overnight: Brewster House B&B, Freeport
Wed, Sep 30 — Southern Maine Coastal Road Trip▶

Freeport → Kennebunkport → York → Nubble → Kittery. This is the day to not rush.

8:00 AM
Breakfast at Brewster House, check out
9:00 AM
Leave Freeport, head south toward Kennebunkport
10:00 AM
Kennebunkport — Dock Square, then scenic Ocean Avenue. Not a shopping stop.
11:45 AM
Lunch: The Clam Shack (on the bridge over the Kennebunk River) — order fried clams & chowder for variety (Alt: The Boathouse)
12:30 PM
Continue south toward York, coastal route
1:00 PM
York Harbor — quick stop, ~30 min
1:45 PM
⭐ Nubble Lighthouse (Sohier Park) — absolutely worth it
  • Spend 45–60 min. Completes the lighthouse trio: Portland Head from water, Portland Head from land, Nubble from the coast
2:45 PM
York Beach / Short Sands Beach — optional; skip if tired. Good ice cream stop if one appears
3:15 PM
Continue to Kittery
3:45 PM
Fort Foster Park — final coastal experience
  • Rocky shoreline, wooded paths, historic fortifications, pier, harbor views
  • Late September is near the end of the seasonal window — verify 2026 gate hours before you go
5:30 PM
Check into Hampton Inn & Suites Kittery-Portsmouth
6:00 PM
Final dinner: Robert's Maine Grill — splurge night (steamed lobster, or haddock/chowder if you're done with lobster), or The Black Birch gastropub for something different
After
Optional: Pepperrell Cove for a short waterfront walk, if you have energy left
🌙 Overnight: Hampton Inn & Suites Kittery-Portsmouth

Top 5 meals of the trip:

  1. Lucky Catch + eating the lobster you caught — the whole experience
  2. Scales — best special dinner (Portland)
  3. Linda Bean's classic lobster roll (Freeport)
  4. Robert's Maine Grill — final dinner (Kittery)
  5. The Clam Shack — fun roadside coastal food (Kennebunkport)

🫐 Leave room for blueberry pie or blueberry ice cream somewhere along the way — it's Maine, you need at least one.
